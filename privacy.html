cwf-backup privacy policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Who uses it. cwf-backup is used only by its owner, to back up the Consumer Welfare Forum research project to the owner's own Google Drive.

What it can access. It asks Google for the drive.file permission only. With that permission it can see and change only the files and folders it creates itself. It cannot read, list or change any other file in the Drive.

What it does with data. It uploads the project's backup files to the owner's Drive and reads them back to check them. It does not collect personal information, does not share any data with anyone, does not use analytics or advertising, and runs only on the owner's computer. Google sign-in tokens are stored only on that computer.

Contact. Questions can be raised through the issues page of this site's repository.
